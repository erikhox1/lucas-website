About the
project

This project is the culmination of four years of mechanical engineering education at Stevens Institute of Technology. The team is designing and fabricating a shockless Mach 14 nozzle for the Stevens shock tunnel, extending its hypersonic research capability using helium as the working fluid.

The work spans analytical gas dynamics, computational fluid dynamics, thermodynamic modeling, precision manufacturing, and systems integration — bringing together the full breadth of the mechanical engineering curriculum in a single, ambitious research project.

Hypersonic Nozzle Project
